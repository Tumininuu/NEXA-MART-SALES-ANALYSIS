## Project Overview

The **NextMart Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a business intelligence project developed to analyze sales performance, product demand, and regional revenue trends across multiple locations and sales representatives.

The dashboard converts raw transactional sales data into interactive visual insights that support strategic decision-making, sales monitoring, and performance evaluation.

The analysis focuses on:

* Revenue performance
* Product sales analysis
* Regional sales distribution
* Sales representative performance
* Category-based sales insights
* Quarterly sales trends
* Quantity sold analysis
# Dashboard Features

## Executive KPI Metrics

The dashboard provides high-level sales performance indicators including:

* Total Revenue
* Total Quantity Sold

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance and sales volume.

# Key Business Insights

## Revenue Performance

* The business generated approximately **₦27 billion** in total revenue.
* Revenue remained strong from **Q2 to Q4**, with a noticeable dip during Q1.
* Quarterly performance showed relatively stable growth across the year.

---

## Product Insights

* Air conditioners generated the highest revenue among all products.
* TVs and laptops also contributed significantly to overall sales performance.
* Electronics remained the dominant product category.

---

## Regional Insights

* The South West region recorded the highest sales revenue.
* North Central generated the lowest revenue among the listed regions.
* Regional sales performance showed moderate variation across locations.

---

## Sales Team Performance

* Revenue contributions across sales representatives remained relatively balanced.
* Sadiq, Blessing, and Ibrahim ranked among the top-performing sales representatives.
